Privacy Policy — Vintage Identifier — Thrift Scanner

This policy covers version 1.1.0 and later. In short: by default, this app collects nothing. There is no account, no backend, no ads, and no tracking. The sections below explain exactly what happens in each mode.

1. Default / offline mode — Data Not Collected

Out of the box, the app works entirely on your device:

  • Photos: garment and tag photos you take stay on your device. They are downscaled and stored only in the app's on-device scan history.
  • Scan history: your identifications, eras, and ranges are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded or synced anywhere.
  • No account: the app has no sign-in, no profile, and no identifier tied to you.
  • No tracking: no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers.

That is why the App Store privacy label for this app reads Data Not Collected — in its default configuration, the app sends no data anywhere.

2. Subscriptions

Unlimited scans are sold as a subscription through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — the app never sees your card details. When a subscription is active, the app checks the subscription status through RevenueCat (the subscription platform) so it knows whether to unlock unlimited scans. RevenueCat receives only what Apple provides for that purpose; it does not receive your photos or scan history.

3. Optional AI vision (only when configured, subscribers only)

If the app has been configured with an AI vision provider, subscribers may have their garment and tag photos sent to that provider (currently OpenAI) so the tag text can be read automatically. This happens only when the feature is configured and you are a subscriber — free scans never send photos anywhere. The provider's own privacy policy governs what it does with images sent to it. The app itself does not store the photos anywhere else, and the era dating still comes from the app's built-in label database, not from the provider.

4. Optional sold-listing prices (only when configured)

If the app has been configured with a sold-listings backend, a price lookup sends only the search terms for the item — brand, garment type, era band, and condition — to that backend so it can fetch recently sold listings from eBay. No photos are sent for price lookups, and no account or personal information is included.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience utility. It collects no data from anyone in its default configuration, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what data the app sends anywhere, this policy will be updated with that version and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
